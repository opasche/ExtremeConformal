# ExtremeConformal 0.2.0

* The article 'Extreme Conformal Prediction: Reliable Intervals for High-Impact Events' describing the methodology implemented in this package has been published in the journal *Extremes*: [Pasche, Lam, and Engelke (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10687-026-00536-9).
* Implementation of weighted conformal prediction intervals for nonexchangeable data, for both extreme conformal and classical conformal methods.
